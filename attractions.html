Must-Visit Attractions

From golden beaches to colonial heritage, explore the diverse landmarks of Negombo.

Nature & Leisure

Negombo Beach Park

The golden sands of Negombo Beach stretch for miles, offering a perfect spot for sunbathing and long walks. The beach park is vibrant with locals and tourists alike, especially during sunset when the sky turns into a canvas of oranges and purples.

  • Best for Sunsets
  • Water Sports Available
  • Beachside Restaurants
Nature & Wildlife

Negombo Lagoon

This massive estuarine lagoon is fed by several small rivers and a canal. It supports a thriving fishing industry and diverse mangrove ecosystem. Take a boat ride to see the traditional outrigger canoes and the rich birdlife.

Did you know?

The lagoon is famous for its crabs and prawns, which you can enjoy fresh at nearby seafood restaurants.

Heritage & Culture

St. Mary's Church

One of the largest cathedrals in Sri Lanka, St. Mary's Church is a testament to the strong Catholic influence in Negombo. Its ceiling paintings are breathtaking, depicting the life of Christ in vivid colors.

Culture & Livelihood

Lellama Fish Market

For an authentic local experience, visit the 'Lellama' early in the morning. Watch the boats return with their catch and witness the bustling auction. It's chaotic, smelly, and absolutely fascinating.

Best time to visit: 5:00 AM - 7:00 AM
