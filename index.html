Discover Negombo

Where History Meets the Ocean

Negombo is a modest beach town located close to Bandaranaike International Airport. With a stash of decent hotels and restaurants to suit all pockets, a friendly local community, an interesting old quarter and a reasonable beach, Negombo is a much easier place to find your feet than Colombo.

Known as "Little Rome" due to its bounty of Catholic churches, the town offers a rich cultural tapestry woven with colonial history and traditional fishing livelihoods.
