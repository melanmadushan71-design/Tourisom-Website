Adventures Await

Make memories with these top-rated experiences in Negombo.

Water 2-3 Hours

Lagoon Boat Safari

Explore the mangroves and witness traditional fishing methods. A peaceful journey through nature's best kept secrets.

Adventure Half Day

Scuba Diving

Discover the 'Third Reef' located 22km offshore. Rich with marine life including rays, fish, and vibrant corals.

Land 4 Hours

City Cycling Tour

Ride through the fishing villages, coconut plantations, and historic canals. A great way to see the real Negombo.

Historical 2 Hours

Hamilton Canal Tour

Glide through the historic Dutch canal network that connects Negombo to Colombo. A serene and scenic route.

Cultural 3 Hours

Seafood Cooking Class

Buy fresh catch from the market and learn to cook traditional Negombo prawn curry with a local family.
